Artistic & Investigative

I create things to satisfy my desire to solve problems and understand what makes things work successfully. I find pleasure in researching how design influences human psychology and drives human intuition. With each creative project I make, I demonstrate how solutions can be unveiled in both ingenious and logical ways. I find value in being a jack of all trades while being a master of one. I strongly believe that, no matter what tool is being used to create something, being a master of the fundamentals of said tool is important in expanding one’s horizons. I’m always eager to learn new tools of the trade to expand my abilities while going back to review the foundations of the concept. Growing up, I was influenced by an era of 3D animation and other digital creations. The accessibility and conveniences of digital software and online resources allowed the basic functionalities to be ingrained into my mind. It got to the point where figuring out how to make actions happen became second nature. I believe that it’s important to have a basic understanding and embrace the rapidly emerging digital era.

A Product of Nassau County

I was born in Eastern New York, but my heart lies within Baldwin, NY, where I've lived for nearly two decades now. I attended Shubert Elementary, Baldwin Middle School, and Baldwin Senior High, which I graduated from in 2015.

Some of my fondest memories are marching in the annual Memorial Day parade, visiting the Baldwin Library after school, making friends in the Baldwin Summer Program, riding my bike up and down the blocks, and visiting Jones Beach.

A Visual Enthusiast

I took an interest in drawing when I was 10 years old when I wanted to draw my favorite Sonic characters. From there, I used online resources to further my knowledge of art fundamentals and digital media products such as Adobe Photoshop. Nowadays, I continue to strengthen my skills and expand my knowledge in visual communication and programming.

When I get to chance, I also like to visit museums in the city such as the MET and MOMA and appreciate the fine arts and events. I've also been growing a collection of web designs I found online that inspire me.

A Dedicated Student

I firmly believe that it's always good to be well-rounded in skills and knowledge. I'm always open to learning new things, and this is especially true when I apply this knowledge to my own projects. I don't believe in settling for the skills you have, but rather improving on them, even a little bit.

Hi, my name is Naomi Clyde. I’m 20 years old and I hail from Nassau County, NY. I transferred to LaGuardia CC from Hunter College in Fall of 2018 in hopes of pursuing a major that is better suited for me. My major is New Media Technology major and I hope to transfer to a 4-year college to further my education.

My interest in digital art dated back in elementary school when I drew in MS Paint with a mouse. I’ve dabbled with Adobe softwares ever since my father introduced me to the Adobe CS4 product, and from there on out my passion for the digital media and design flourished.

When I’m not doing school work or enhancing my digital skills, I like to draw with friends, make and edit pictures, play video games, and visit museums in Manhattan. In my spare time, I’m also teaching myself how to animate.
